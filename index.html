The Past

Where I started.

Growing up meant living in a household where technology was slowly shifting from a rare novelty to a daily fixture. The home revolved around a single, bulky desktop computer resting on a shared wooden desk. Family life often intersected around that machine—whether it was a parent balancing work spreadsheets in the evening, siblings waiting their turn to play basic games, or everyone gathering to look up a question in a digital encyclopedia.

The Present

Where I am now.

A first-year IT student specializing in Network and Security, building my foundation in network architectures, cybersecurity protocols, and infrastructure defense.
